    Find the value of a formula by substitution.

    Replace each letter with its number, then work it out one line at a time, just like in your homework. Six question types, from simple formulas to negatives and squares.

    How it works

    • The first line is the substitution: write the formula with the numbers in place of the letters. Each line after starts with =.
    • Correct line: +10. A line with the wrong value: −10 and you get a hint.
    • If your line has the right value but isn’t the step asked for, you get a tip and lose nothing.
    • Put negative numbers in brackets, like 3 × (−4). The fraction key divides, and shows 6 ÷ 2 as a fraction.
    • Make a mistake and you’ll answer one more question of the same type. Finish a question with no mistakes to clear the type. Clear all 6 to win the trophy.
    Your line

    The fraction key divides: 6, fraction, 2 gives 6 over 2. For a fraction with a sum on top, put the top in brackets first. Keyboard works too: / for fractions, ^ for powers, Enter to check.
